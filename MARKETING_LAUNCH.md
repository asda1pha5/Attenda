## First Pinterest Pin

**Image:** `public/pinterest-baby-shower-rsvp.png`

Add this text in Canva over the open upper portion of the image:

```text

```

Small line below it:

```text
Plan, invite, and keep every yes in one place.
```

## Posting cadence

Make three Pins from the same page over the next two weeks. Change only the headline and image treatment:

1. `A beautiful baby shower RSVP link`
2. `Stop chasing baby shower RSVPs by text`
3. `Everything your baby shower guests need in one link`

Each Pin points to the same focused landing page. This lets the landing page, not a DM conversation, do the selling.
